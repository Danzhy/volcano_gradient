Why flock moves to the right?
1. Agents are initialized heading to the right.
2. The territory outside of the map is white, which repels agents
3. There are enough agents so the white zone outside of the map borders repels those agents strong enough

A special case: When heading is initialized to the left or randomly, and the number of agents is small, then agents might drift
out of the map's bounds. Thus, points 1,2,3 are important.



Initial heading of the agents is set in the initialization of attributes of FlockingUtils2DWithLightSensor class
